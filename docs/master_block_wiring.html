3. Port Pinouts & Electrical Subsystems

4-Pin Magnetic Pogo Connectors

Standardized non-overlapping pin order shared between Config Dock, Run Port, and Inter-Block links.

Battery & DC-DC Power Flow

BMS-protected Li-ion flow stepped down/up to a rock-solid 3.3V rail to prevent RF dropouts.

[18650 Cell: 3.7V] │ ▼ [TP4056 Charger] ◄── [USB-C 5V Input] │ (OUT+) [Slide Switch] │ (VIN & EN) [TPS63020 Buck-Boost] (PS -> GND) │ (VOUT = 3.3V) ──────┴───────────────────────────── +3.3V SYSTEM POWER BUS (To ESP32-S3)
Design Note: Tying EN → VIN on the TPS63020 powers off both the regulator and ESP32 when switched off, with zero standby quiescent drain.
4. Assembly Checklist & Hardware Rules
  • ✔
    Strict 3.3V DC Domain: Verify with a digital multimeter that the VCC rail never exceeds 3.35V. Connecting 5V directly to the E-Ink display or CH32V003 pins will damage the silicon.
  • ✔
    10 kΩ Bus Pull-Up Resistors: Place a 10 kΩ resistor between GPIO 15 / GPIO 16 and +3.3V rail. This prevents floating UART lines and spurious packet interrupts when blocks are disconnected.
  • ✔
    0.1 μF Decoupling Capacitors: Place 100 nF ceramic capacitors right across the V+ and GND pins of the Run Chain and Config Dock sockets to eliminate contact bounce when snapping blocks together.
  • ✔
    Start Button Diagonal Wiring: Ensure the orange lead (GPIO 14) and black ground lead are placed diagonally across the 12×12mm switch terminals to avoid connecting two permanently bridged legs.
